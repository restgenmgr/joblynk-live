To pass ATS filters: **✅ Do:** Use standard section headings ("Work Experience," not "My Jobs") **✅ Do:** Include keywords from the job description (e.g., "Opera PMS," "guest recovery") **❌ Don't:** Use tables, graphics, or unusual fonts **❌ Don't:** Save as .docx if PDF is accepted ## Follow-Up Email Template (After Application) **Subject:** Following up on [Job Title] application – [Your Name] Dear [Hiring Manager Name], I submitted my application for the [Job Title] position on [Date]. I wanted to reiterate my strong interest in joining the team at [Property Name]. With my background in [key skill], I am confident I can contribute to your guest service standards. Thank you for your time and consideration. Sincerely, [Your Name] [Phone] ## Red Flags to Avoid - ⚠️ Job postings asking for upfront payment - ⚠️ Recruiters promising guaranteed placement for a fee - ⚠️ Salary offers far above market rate - ⚠️ Emails from free domains (Gmail) claiming to be from HR ## Ready to Apply?
